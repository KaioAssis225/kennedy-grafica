## Platform

web

## Stack

Single static page — plain HTML + CSS, no framework and no backend. Confirmed as a durable constraint: the site stays informational and does not take orders or payments online. One small dependency (Leaflet + OpenStreetMap) powers the store-location map. Google Fonts are loaded remotely.

## Users

Primary: **walk-in individuals in and around Indaiatuba/SP** who need something printed or a photo done quickly, or need school/office supplies. Typical jobs: color or black-and-white prints, ID photos (3x4, 5x5, 7x5 "na hora"), photo revelation/printing (10x15 up to custom sizes), single copies, and stationery.

Secondary: **local businesses (B2B)** needing business cards, cut-vinyl and adhesive signage for vehicles/windows/storefronts, labels, banners, and large-format plotting. The shop explicitly offers "atendimento para empresas."

Situation: the visitor wants a nearby, reliable shop and a fast answer. They arrive on the site to confirm the shop does what they need, then either message on WhatsApp or walk into the store.

## Product Purpose

A one-page marketing/lead-generation site for **Kennedy Gráfica Rápida & Papelaria**, a physical quick-print shop and stationery store in Indaiatuba/SP. It exists to convert local intent into contact: showcase the full range of services, then drive the visitor to WhatsApp or an in-person visit. This site replaces the business's previous Google Sites page as the real, published website.

Success = more qualified WhatsApp conversations started and more people walking into the store, having already seen that the shop offers the service they need.

## Positioning

A single neighborhood shop that covers an unusually broad range under one roof — from same-hour ID photos and photo printing to business cards, cut-vinyl signage, labels, banners, and large-format plotting (A2, A1, metro linear) — with fast turnaround ("rápida") and both presential and WhatsApp service. The defensible facts a competitor cannot truthfully copy: the established physical location (Av. Pres. Kennedy, 826, Indaiatuba), the "na hora" speed on photos, and the specific in-house service breadth.

## Operating Context

- Customers reach the shop two ways: a pre-filled WhatsApp message (wa.me/551938251616) or by visiting the physical store.
- Orders and quotes are handled by a person over WhatsApp or at the counter — there is no online cart, upload, or checkout.
- The site provides directions via an interactive map (Leaflet + OpenStreetMap) with a "Como Chegar" action and a Google Maps directions link.

## Product Principles

1. **WhatsApp-first contact.** The shortest path from "I need this" to a real person answering is the primary conversion; keep that action obvious and everywhere it's useful.
2. **Local and presential.** Anchor everything in Indaiatuba and the physical store — address, hours, and directions are core content, not footnotes. The site brings people to the counter or the chat; it does not transact.
3. **Broad range, made scannable.** One shop covers many services, from 3x4 photos to large-format plotting. Make the full breadth obvious fast without overwhelming the visitor.
4. **Speed is the promise.** "Rápida" and "na hora" are the brand's core claim; reinforce fast turnaround throughout.
5. **Real facts stay real.** Address, CNPJ, hours, and phone are factual and must remain accurate; never invent reviews, pricing, or capabilities.
